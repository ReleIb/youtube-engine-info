UBT Stories / YouTube Engine is a locally operated video production and publishing tool. It helps its operator prepare content, review videos and connect the YouTube channels they own or are authorized to manage.

03 / PUBLISH

Use the right channel

Upload approved content to the channel selected and authorized by the operator, and review available performance information.

Google & YouTube connection

Permission comes from the channel owner

The application uses Google OAuth and YouTube API Services. Depending on the permissions granted, it can identify the connected channel, read video information, upload videos and associated publishing details, and retrieve channel or video analytics, including revenue information when authorized.

The Google consent screen lists the permissions being requested. The operator authorizes each channel connection separately. A shared application identity and this same website can describe multiple owner-authorized channels.

The engine runs on the operator's computer. This public website provides application and privacy information; it does not provide a dashboard login or receive YouTube authorization tokens.

Privacy & control

Your connection stays under your control

Read the Privacy Policy for information about data access, local storage, service providers and deletion. Google access can be revoked from Google Account connections.

This tool is independently operated and is not a Google or YouTube product. Use of its YouTube integration is subject to the YouTube Terms of Service.
